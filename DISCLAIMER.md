## Informational purposes only

This project is a date-logic tool and a learning exercise. It is NOT
an official collection schedule, and it is NOT advice from the City
of Montreal, the City of Longueuil, or any waste-collection service.

It does not tell you your official pickup day. It tells you what
*your configured sector* says, based on the rules you entered.

## Not official, not affiliated, not endorsed

This is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the City of Montreal, the City
of Longueuil, or any borough, contractor, or collection service.

The sample sectors (`plateau-sample`, `longueuil-sample`) are
examples in the shape of real rules. They are NOT official schedules
for any real address. Schedules vary by sector and change for
holidays, seasonal rules, November biweekly garbage in some areas,
July/August green-waste exceptions, and service changes.

## Verify before you put bins out

Before relying on this tool for a real pickup, look up your address
on montreal.ca (Montreal) or longueuil.quebec / 311 (Longueuil),
and copy your real days into a sector of your own. If this tool and
the official schedule disagree, the official schedule wins — every
time.
